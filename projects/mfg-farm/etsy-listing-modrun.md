# ModRun Cable Management System — Listing Copy

## Product Overview (for reference)

ModRun is a 5-piece modular cable management family:
- **Rail (desk-edge C-clamp variant)** — clamps onto desk edge 15–30mm thick, no adhesive needed
- **Rail (adhesive-pad variant)** — mounts to any flat surface via 3M adhesive pads
- **Clip 3mm** — for thin cables (USB-C, phone chargers, earbuds)
- **Clip 6mm** — for medium cables (laptop power, HDMI, USB-A)
- **Clip 12mm** — for thick cables (power strips, monitor cables, extension cords)

Clips press-fit into the rail's slots at 30mm spacing; remove and reposition without tools.
